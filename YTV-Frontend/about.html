A Movement Born From Youth Ambition

Y-TV (Youth Transformation for Vision 2060) is a national youth-driven movement committed to empowering young people, transforming local communities, and establishing a prosperous Kenya by the year 2060.

Our Vision

A transformed Kenya by 2060 - driven by empowered, innovative, and patriotic youth. Envisioning a future where every young person has the resources, voice, and leadership mindset to champion sustainable development across the nation.

Our Mission

To empower young people to realize their potential and actively contribute to Kenya's transformation by 2060. Equipping youth through capacity building, leadership mentorship, advocacy, and direct community transformation initiatives.

Our Brand Personality

Energetic & Passionate

Driven by the vibrant spirit and enthusiasm of young Kenyans who are eager to lead and innovate.

Patriotic & Visionary

Deeply rooted in national pride, aiming toward the prosperous milestone of Kenya Vision 2060.

Inclusive & Empowering

Welcoming youth from all backgrounds, regions, and socio-economic spheres to unite as one force.
